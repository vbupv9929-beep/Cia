TO MY CIA

Cia ♡

Cia,

Sometimes I try to find the right words to explain what you mean to me, but somehow every word feels too small.

I love having you in my life. I love the way your name alone can make me smile, and how even the smallest things about you can stay in my mind for hours.

I love your little details, your laugh, the way you talk, and all those simple things you probably don't even realize are special. Somehow, they became some of my favorite things.

Being close to you made me realize that someone can slowly become such a beautiful part of your everyday life that you can't imagine your days without them.

And if you ever ask me what I love most about you, I honestly don't think I could choose just one thing.

Maybe it's everything.
Your personality, your smile, your little habits, your presence, and the way you make ordinary moments feel different. 🎀

I don't want to make huge promises with complicated words. I just want you to know that you are genuinely precious to me, more than I can properly explain.

And whenever you read this, I hope you smile for a moment and remember that somewhere, someone thinks you're incredibly special.

Cia, I like you more than these words could ever explain. 🎀🤍
